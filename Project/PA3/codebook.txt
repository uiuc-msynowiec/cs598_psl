# UNGA voting extracts — codebook

Source: Erik Voeten, Anton Strezhnev, and Michael Bailey,
"United Nations General Assembly Voting Data",
Harvard Dataverse, https://doi.org/10.7910/DVN/LEJUQZ
via the public TidyTuesday / `unvotes` compilation
(https://github.com/rfordatascience/tidytuesday/tree/main/data/2021/2021-03-23).

Absences and non-memberships are already omitted from the long table
(only recorded yes / no / abstain votes appear). Wide matrices therefore
contain blanks when a member did not record a vote on that resolution.

## Files

| File | Contents |
|---|---|
| `votes_windowA.csv` | Country × resolution votes, calendar years 2010–2013. 192 countries, 340 resolutions. |
| `votes_windowB.csv` | Same layout, calendar years 2016–2019. 192 countries, 428 resolutions. |
| `resolutions_windowA.csv` | `rcid`, session, date, UN resolution symbol, short title for window A. |
| `resolutions_windowB.csv` | Same for window B. |
| `country_labels.csv` | `country`, `country_code` (ISO-2), `un_regional_group`, flags for window membership. |

`un_regional_group` is an **interpretation label**. It is not a feature.
The five official UN regional groups are African, Asia-Pacific, Eastern
European, GRULAC (Latin American and Caribbean), and WEOG (Western
European and Others). Turkey and Israel are coded WEOG; Cyprus is
Asia-Pacific, matching official practice. The grouping is a snapshot
and is not a claim about alliances.

## Vote columns

In the vote files the first two columns are `country` and `country_code`.
Every later column is named `rcid_<id>` and takes one of

- `yes`
- `no`
- `abstain`
- blank (no recorded vote)

## Required roster

These ten countries appear in both windows. Use them in the named-unit table:

United States, Russia, China, India, Brazil, South Africa, Israel, Iran, Ukraine, Turkey.
